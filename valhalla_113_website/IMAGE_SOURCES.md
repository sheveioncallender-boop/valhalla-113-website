Native product cards always use the real product records' images. Replace decorative shots with owner-supplied venue/food photographs as available. Do not present stock interiors or stock crowds as photos taken at Valhalla.

Social/event artwork is reproduced at the owner's request for their venue and links to its original publication; it is not covered by this addon's software license. No outdated admission prices, specials or recurring schedule are re-presented as current offers. Full-resolution Facebook venue photos were unavailable through public access during this update; the gallery therefore contains verifiable past-event artwork, not an invented venue photo collection.
